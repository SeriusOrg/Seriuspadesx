# Changes

What each release brings, shortest first. The release workflow puts a
version's section at the top of its release notes, under "What's new", and
the game shows those lines when it offers the update. Keep each line short:
one change, in a player's words.

## 0.3.8

- New: the horde plays the map you pick - a button under Tower Defense
  lists every map in the Maps folder, the mode's own course at the top
- New: the /va chat command in a horde world says which map stands next -
  leave to the menu and press Tower Defense to play it
- Improved: the walking dead cross the land better - vaulting low walls,
  climbing the stairs they dig, detouring around a maze that beats them
- New: a map's settings file can name its zombie fronts and its core, and
  put each named spawn site on the floor it asks for
- Fixed: those settings read again, written plain or quoted, and the
  server says which map and settings it opened
- New: the Zedcred runs the horde - kills, waves and sentries pay, the
  prices ride the tuning file, and the purse opens at three turrets
- New: five brutes walk the waves, each in its own skin and manner - the
  Tank swells a wave at a time, the Healer mends its pack, the Spitter
  lobs its acid pool, the Breaker digs its straight road to the core,
  the Leaper walks with the pack but pounces the player it hunts
- New: the Boomer waddles in bladders that blink ever faster and burst
  like a grenade, and its burst wears a headshot geyser
- New: every brute stands marked in its own colour, seen through walls
- New: the record book keeps each map its best wave, and the menu names
  the map and its record
- Changed: the waves never end - they climb five by five, then ten, then
  twenty, and the brutes ride the climb

## 0.3.7

- New: the wheel opens on a Defence page - build, aim and clear the turrets
  and the wave from the middle mouse button (horde)
- Improved: the dead honour the maze - a pack sealed at a wall digs a way
  through it at last, stairs and all, and only when its legs fail it
- New: the campaign has an end - the last wave cleared, at twenty, is the
  victory
- New: zombies read as gray circles on the maps, whichever side watches

## 0.3.6

- New: a headshot kill bursts the skull into pieces (Options, Effects to turn it off)
- Improved: fewer and smaller particles on the Quest
- Fixed: zombies' right arms and legs, and one zombie's chest
- New: zombies hold their own pickaxe as well as their spade

## 0.3.5

- New: zombies come in several looks, and each kind of brute has its own
- New: zombies and brutes carry their own spade, and the Spitter its acid
- New: smoke clouds and coloured smoke markers on servers that use them
- New: a model editor, sharper text on high-DPI screens, and automatic screen setup
- New: Escape on the main screen asks before quitting

## 0.3.4

- Improved: smoothness on servers with up to 200 players
- Fixed: flashlight disabled on some graphics settings
- Improved: cleaner colours, with the colour filter off on every graphics setting
- New: icons in the server list for each server's extra features; hover a server to see what they are
- New: an Extensions filter in the server list
- Fixed: some server features named "Unknown" in the pause menu

## 0.3.3

- Training has its own page: the Tutorial and Aim training
- Aim training: Gridshot, Flick, Tracking, Strafe duel and Long range, with scores and your bests
- The tutorial shows where to go next, with a checklist and a timer
- Ammo, grenades and blocks never run out in Training
- VR players crouch instead of folding over, with thicker arms and hands that turn
- VR: the kill feed is back, in front of you
- VR: block colours can be picked on the wrist menu again
- A mod that breaks the scripts is turned off, so it can always be unticked
- The update prompt shows what the new version brings

## 0.3.2

- Fixed a crash on Mac when a mod's sound could not be read
- A sound that cannot be loaded now plays as silence instead of breaking a weapon

## 0.3.1

- The game updates itself on Windows and Linux: click Update Now
- Linux: the package now includes the game's data

## 0.3.0

- First release of ZeroSpades X: VR on PC and Meta Quest 3, and Training
